Five declarations are recorded. `matrixApply_eq_booleanListSum` states that matrix application is a Boolean sum of column entries weighted by the argument, which converts the matrix action into the sum calculus the development already has. `booleanListSum_and_left` and `booleanListSum_and_right` distribute a constant `&&` out of such a sum on either side. `supportBasisColumnN` defines the full `n`-indexed columns of the basis matrix, `supportBasisMatrix_apply_column` proves the matrix columns are those columns, and `transpose_supportBasisColumnN` proves the transformed full column is the paired-block extension of the corresponding unit tuple, or zero past the pair count. The first three use no axioms, the last two use `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, and none depends on `sorryAx`.

This is groundwork, not the action identity. The identity `matrixApply D (extendCoordinates y) = kernelCoordinateElement y` is **not** proved here, and neither is the annihilator consequence nor the matrix form of the support statement. The remaining step is the coupled double sum: pulling the constant tuple entry out of the inner sum requires reassociating inside the summand before distributing, and that step did not close in this attempt. So the sum calculus and the column transforms are in place and verified, the main composition is still missing, and the computability clause still has no bit-operation cost trace. The matched-sector proposition, branches, discovery, moments, estimator and family propositions have no coverage, and no published theorem is certified. These are helper results; nothing here establishes quantum advantage or a complexity consequence.
