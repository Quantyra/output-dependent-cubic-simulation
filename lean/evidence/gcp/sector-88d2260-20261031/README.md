Three declarations are recorded here. `rowOperationCNOTs_length_le` bounds a single row operation at three CNOT records. `rowProgramCNOTs_length_le` bounds a whole row program's gate list at three times its operation count. `gaussianInverseApply_gate_count_le` composes that with the existing `gaussianRowProgram_length` bound to give at most three times the dimension times the dimension plus one many gates for the inverse that binary elimination runs. All three use `propext` and `Quot.sound`, and none depends on `sorryAx`.

This is the first certified cost statement for a computed object in this family. It is an operation count in the same style as the existing `PolarCost.lean` bounds on the elimination's program length, and it measures the gate sequence the executable inverse applies.

It is not the bit-cost bound the manuscript's computability clause requires, and this evidence must not be read as supplying it. `lean/SEMANTICS.md` requires a cost trace in bit operations across parsing and replay, elimination, tensor and phase substitution, candidate enumeration, random-bit draws, real quadratic Gauss sums, integer arithmetic, accumulation and rounding, with source size, dimensions, precision and confidence tracked. What is bounded here is one gate count for one of those steps, with no bit-width accounting and no coverage of the other steps. The clause also covers the support, its origin and the sign, whose computations are built but whose costs are not bounded. Separately, the rank-evenness, support-dimension, magnitude and quadratic-sign clauses are each proved in the formalization's own terms and remain blocked on the rank identification recorded in `lean/SEMANTICS.md`. The normalized sector identity and denominator, matched-sector boundedness, complete costs and every labeled paper theorem remain uncertified. These are helper results; no published manuscript theorem is certified here, and nothing here establishes quantum advantage or a complexity consequence.
