One declaration is recorded here. `sourceWalshSum_support_natAbs` proves that a nonzero exact source Walsh value has magnitude exactly two to the power of the dimension less the recorded polar-elimination pair count. It restricts the already-certified canonical magnitude identity to the support: that identity gives either zero or two to the power of the radical dimension plus the pair count, and nonvanishing selects the second branch, which the recorded dimension witness rewrites into the stated exponent. It uses `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, and does not depend on `sorryAx`.

Because the recorded pair count is half the paired block size established at `13348ec`, dividing by two to the dimension turns this into a normalized magnitude of two to the minus half that block size. That is the shape of the manuscript's magnitude clause, which asserts that every nonzero normalized value has magnitude two to the minus half the polar rank.

The clause is nevertheless not certified, and this evidence must not be read as certifying it. The exponent here is the recorded pair count, a quantity of the elimination's output; the manuscript's exponent is half the rank of the alternating polar form. Those agree only once the rank is identified with twice the pair count, which is exactly the open modelling decision recorded in `lean/SEMANTICS.md` under "Rank of the polar form: open modelling decision" and not yet taken. The same gap blocks the rank-evenness and support-dimension clauses. The remaining clauses are untouched: the quadratic sign on the support, and computability of the support, its origin and that sign by binary elimination. The correspondence between the formalized source Walsh sum and the manuscript's Walsh transform of the output-dependent quadratic is also not established. The normalized sector identity and denominator, matched-sector boundedness, complete costs and every labeled paper theorem remain uncertified. These are helper results; no published manuscript theorem is certified here, and nothing here establishes quantum advantage or a complexity consequence.
