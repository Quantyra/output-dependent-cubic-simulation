Three declarations are recorded here. `pairCoefficientsXor_replicate_false` is the pair analogue of the existing radical coefficient law: xoring a pair-coefficient list with the all-false list of the same length is the identity. `hyperbolicSignature_vectorPairAssignment` evaluates the hyperbolic signature of a coordinate pair assignment as the parity of the coordinatewise products, and depends on no axioms at all. `sourceWalshSupportSign_as_quadratic` combines them to restate the support sign as the recorded constant xored with the parity of the products of the canonical left and right coordinates of the transformed character. The first and third use `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; none depends on `sorryAx`.

The sign is therefore an explicit quadratic form: a constant plus a sum of products of pairs of coordinates. With the support coordinates fixed at `6e62752`, this is the quadraticity the manuscript's sign clause describes, expressed in the formalization's own coordinates.

The clause is still not certified, and this evidence must not be read as certifying it. Two gaps remain specific to it. Beyond that, the manuscript states quadraticity against its own support basis matrix, and relating that to either of the formalization's coordinate systems needs the rank identification recorded in `lean/SEMANTICS.md`. The rank-evenness, support-dimension and magnitude clauses remain blocked on the same identification. The computability clause has its subject attached and its constructions built but no bit-cost bound.
