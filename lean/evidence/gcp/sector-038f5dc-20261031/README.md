Two declarations are recorded here, with the supporting definitions `recordedResidualPreimage` and `sourceWalshSupportSign`. `recordedResidualPreimage` computes a preimage of a residual target under the residual projection: it places the target in the canonical radical block, maps through the interleaved layout, casts back along the dimension witness, reindexes by the computed coordinate inverse and applies the executable Gaussian inverse. `recordedResidualPreimage_correct` proves the projection of that preimage is the given target, assuming the matrix action is injective and the recorded pairs are distinct and pairwise disjoint. `sourceWalshSupportSign` packages the sign of a nonzero exact source Walsh coefficient as a computation, and `sourceWalshSum_support_value_as_sign` restates the exact coefficient value through it. Both theorems use `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, and neither depends on `sorryAx`.

Together with the executable Gaussian inverse at `f932679` and the computed coordinate inverse at `4aa343d`, every ingredient the manuscript's computability clause needs for the support and the sign is now not only proved but computed: residual preimages are produced by elimination and lookup rather than obtained from existence proofs, and the sign is a named function with a correctness theorem.

The clause is still not certified, and this evidence must not be read as certifying it. Two things are missing. Second, the clause's force in the manuscript is partly a cost statement, and no bit-cost bound for these computations is proved here. Separately, the magnitude, rank-evenness and support-dimension clauses remain blocked on the rank identification recorded in `lean/SEMANTICS.md`, and the quadratic-sign clause on the support-coordinate bridge. The normalized sector identity and denominator, matched-sector boundedness, complete costs and every labeled paper theorem remain uncertified.
