Two declarations are recorded here. `two_lt_two_pow_succ` proves two is strictly below two to the dimension plus one whenever the dimension is at least one. `familyProbabilityBound_scaled` is the scaled form of the manuscript's displayed family probability bound: the manuscript's value, one quarter less two to the minus dimension minus one plus two to the minus twice the dimension minus one, is strictly below one quarter, and multiplying through by two to the twice-dimension-plus-two clears denominators and leaves the stated integer inequality, which holds for every dimension at least one. Both use `propext` and `Quot.sound`, and neither depends on `sorryAx`.

The scope of this evidence is narrow and should not be overstated. It certifies the arithmetic step of the displayed bound only. It does not identify the family's output probability with the manuscript's value: that identification is the substance of the proposition and requires the output-probability semantics, the family's construction, its support and rank claims and its control count, none of which is certified. This is the first declaration in the development that touches the growing-family proposition at all, and it touches only the final inequality of its proof.
