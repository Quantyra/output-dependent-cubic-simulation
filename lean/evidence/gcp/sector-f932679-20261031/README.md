Three declarations are recorded here, with the supporting definition `gaussianInverseApply`. That definition is the executable inverse of an invertible binary matrix action: it looks up the matrix's Gaussian row program and runs the corresponding CNOT sequence, falling back to the identity when no program exists. `gaussianInverseApply_forward` proves it is a right inverse whenever the matrix action is injective. `polarEliminate_transpose_inverseApply` instantiates that at the transpose of the polar-elimination matrix, whose injectivity comes from the earlier surjectivity results. `residualKernel_explicit_preimage` then exhibits the residual kernel element realizing a given paired-block target as the value of this executable inverse, together with its kernel membership. All three use `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`, and none depends on `sorryAx`.

The point is the change in character rather than new mathematics: where the earlier parametrization asserted that a unique kernel element exists, the element is now produced by running binary elimination. The definition itself is executable; the `Classical.choice` in the axiom sets enters through the surrounding injectivity and program-existence lemmas, not through the construction.

The manuscript's computability clause is not certified, and this evidence must not be read as certifying it. That clause asserts the support, its origin and the quadratic sign are computable by binary elimination. What is certified here covers preimages for the kernel. The support origin additionally requires solving against the residual offset, and the construction currently available for that routes through `coordinateInverse` of the recorded coordinate map, which is obtained from a surjectivity proof rather than computed; making the origin executable needs a computable inverse of that permutation, which the recorded coordinate order admits but which is not yet built. The sign is already a closed expression in computable terms but has not been packaged as a computation with a correctness statement. The magnitude, rank-evenness and support-dimension clauses remain blocked on the rank identification recorded in `lean/SEMANTICS.md`, and the quadratic-sign clause on the support-coordinate bridge. The correspondence between the formalized source Walsh sum and the manuscript's Walsh transform of the output-dependent quadratic is also not established. The normalized sector identity and denominator, matched-sector boundedness, complete costs and every labeled paper theorem remain uncertified. These are helper results; no published manuscript theorem is certified here, and nothing here establishes quantum advantage or a complexity consequence.
